🔍 What is Guardian?

Guardian is a lightweight USB security utility for Windows. It does not replace your antivirus—it complements it.

While traditional antiviruses scan the whole system, Guardian sits quietly in the background, watching your USB ports, Desktop, and Downloads folder. It catches threats before they execute, giving you full control.

  • USB Virus Scanner: Instantly scans any USB drive for threats the moment it is inserted.
  • USB History Tracker: Logs every USB ever plugged in (Drive, Time, Volume Label, File System).
  • Real-Time Monitor: Watches your Desktop, Downloads, and Temp folders for suspicious files.
  • Quarantine & Exceptions: Move threats to a secure folder, or whitelist files you trust.
  • Live Threat Counter: Shows exactly how many threats Guardian has blocked.

📖 How to Use Guardian

Step 1: Download the `.exe` file (from the button below).

Step 2: Right-click the file and select "Run as administrator" (required for full USB detection).

Step 3: Insert a USB drive. Guardian will detect it and ask if you want to scan it.

Step 4: Check the "USB History" tab to see a log of all USBs ever connected.

Step 5: If a threat is found, you can Quarantine it or Trust it (add to exceptions).
